# References

## Models implemented

- **Continuum.** D. A. Spong, R. Sanchez and A. Weller, "Shear Alfvén continua
  in stellarators", *Phys. Plasmas* **10**, 3217–3224 (2003).
  [doi:10.1063/1.1590316](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1590316)
- **Global operator.** D. A. Spong, E. D'Azevedo and Y. Todo, "Clustered
  frequency analysis of shear Alfvén modes in stellarators", *Phys. Plasmas*
  **17**, 022106 (2010). [doi:10.1063/1.3313818](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3313818)
- **Reduced ordering and energy principle.** S. E. Kruger, C. C. Hegna and
  J. D. Callen, "Generalized reduced magnetohydrodynamic equations", *Phys.
  Plasmas* **5**, 4169–4182 (1998).
  [doi:10.1063/1.873152](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.873152); J. P. Freidberg,
  *Ideal MHD* (Cambridge University Press, 2014), the $\delta W$ form used for
  the finite-$\beta$ terms.
- **Continuum of quasisymmetric stellarators, gap widths, objectives.**
  E. J. Paul, A. Hyder, E. Rodríguez, R. Jorge and A. Knyazev, "The shear
  Alfvén continuum of quasisymmetric stellarators", *J. Plasma Phys.* **91**,
  E101 (2025). [doi:10.1017/S0022377825100524](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0022377825100524)
- **Stellarator gaps.** Ya. I. Kolesnichenko, V. V. Lutsenko, H. Wobig,
  Yu. V. Yakovenko and O. P. Fesenyuk, "Alfvén continuum and high-frequency
  eigenmodes in optimized stellarators", *Phys. Plasmas* **8**, 491–509 (2001).
  [doi:10.1063/1.1339228](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1339228)
- **Drive.** L. Chen and F. Zonca, "Physics of Alfvén waves and energetic
  particles in burning plasmas", *Rev. Mod. Phys.* **88**, 015008 (2016).
  [doi:10.1103/RevModPhys.88.015008](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.88.015008)
- W. W. Heidbrink, "Basic physics of Alfvén instabilities driven by energetic
  particles in toroidally confined plasmas", *Phys. Plasmas* **15**, 055501
  (2008). [doi:10.1063/1.2838239](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.2838239)
- G. Y. Fu and J. W. Van Dam, *Phys. Fluids B* **1**, 1949 (1989), and
  R. Betti and J. P. Freidberg, *Phys. Fluids B* **4**, 1465 (1992):
  the Maxwellian TAE drive and Landau damping scaling.
